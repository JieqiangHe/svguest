A portable SVG editor

Another roof, another figure.

A tiny SVG editor for scientific figures, in one HTML file. Touch up figures from ggplot2, Inkscape or Adobe Illustrator, right in your browser.

or press Enter

Runs in your browser or as a TBtools plugin, where SVG Chat can use TBtools' model (no API key) or your own paid ChatGPT plan, and uploads nothing: only what you send to SVG Chat leaves your computer.

Behind the name

“Another roof, another proof.”

Paul Erdős, 1913–1996

Erdős travelled with a suitcase, staying with fellow mathematicians and working on proofs together. A different roof, another collaboration. svguest borrows that spirit: a small guest that moves in for a while, as a tab in your browser or a plugin in TBtools, and works on your figures with you.

This little tool may not last long. His story deserves to.
